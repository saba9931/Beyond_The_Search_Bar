# Literature Review --- GEO and AI Beauty Discovery

**Version:** 0.1 starter synthesis, 14 October 2026. This is not an
exhaustive systematic review; update the search log and source matrix
before submission.

## Review question

What does research and official guidance establish about visibility in
AI-generated answers, how can it be measured, and what can public
observations tell us about Sephora India?

## 1. Foundational GEO research

Aggarwal et al. (2024), *GEO: Generative Engine Optimization* (KDD 2024;
https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.09735) formalises GEO and introduces
GEO-bench. The paper reports visibility gains for tested strategies in
its experimental setting and finds effects vary by domain. It supports
measuring visibility and testing content strategies rather than relying
on anecdotes. Its reported gains must not be assumed to transfer to
Sephora India, current AI products, organic discovery, or revenue.

## 2. Official platform guidance

Google Search Central (2026), *Google's guide to optimizing for
generative AI features on Google Search*
(https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide)
says foundational SEO remains relevant to Google's AI features. It
emphasises useful, original, reliable content and technical eligibility,
warns against unsupported GEO "hacks," and notes that inclusion is not
guaranteed. This is primary guidance for Google Search only, not proof
about ChatGPT or Gemini.

## 3. Critical survey of GEO

Martinez (2026), *Optimizing Visibility in Generative Engines: A
Critical Survey of Generative Engine Optimization (2023--2026)*
(https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14035) reviews 45 studies and frames GEO as
a multi-stage, partly observable process involving discovery, retrieval,
ranking/context allocation, citation, prominence and user behaviour. It
notes heterogeneous metrics, variability and limited evidence for stable
cross-platform causal effects on organic discovery or downstream
outcomes. Check preprint status and review method before presenting it
as peer-reviewed consensus.

## 4. Sephora AI commerce

Sephora (2026, March 24), *Sephora App in ChatGPT Brings a New
Personalized Beauty Experience*
(https://newsroom.sephora.com/sephora-app-in-chatgpt-brings-a-new-personalized-beauty-experience/?version=published)
announced a ChatGPT app for curated advice and product discovery,
initially piloted in the United States. This establishes AI-commerce
activity, not Sephora India's organic recommendation share or an
India-specific visibility gap. It is a company announcement, not an
independent impact evaluation.

## 5. Indian business context

Reliance Industries Limited (2023, November 3), *Sephora ties up with
Reliance Retail Ventures Limited to transform India's prestige beauty
retail segment*
(https://www.ril.com/news-media/press-releases/sephora-ties-rrvl-transform-indias-prestige-beauty-retail-segment)
documents the partnership and rights to develop Sephora's India presence
across channels. It is business context, not evidence of AI visibility
or standalone India revenue.

## 6. Industry measurement examples

-   Cited Index (2026), *Best Skincare & Beauty in AI Search India,
    August 2026* (https://www.getcited.in/cited-index/skincare-beauty):
    uses non-branded consumer prompts across five AI platforms and
    publishes mention/position/citation measures. Useful as a benchmark,
    but it is a commercial index; inspect sampling, weights, platform
    versions and conflicts.
-   AccessAegis (2026, August 29), *AI Visibility in India: 2026 Brand
    Study* (https://accessaegis.com/research/ai-visibility-india-2026/):
    describes 15 buyer prompts per brand across ChatGPT and Perplexity,
    saving answers and URLs. Useful as a protocol example;
    vendor-produced and may not estimate repeat-run variation if each
    prompt is run once.

## Synthesis: established vs. not established

**Better supported:** AI-answer visibility can be operationalised with
explicit mention, position and citation metrics; GEO has been tested
experimentally; official Google guidance emphasises helpful content and
existing SEO foundations; platforms and responses may vary; Sephora has
announced AI-enabled commerce in ChatGPT for an initial US pilot.

**Not established:** Sephora India has a visibility gap versus
Nykaa/Tira; any one factor causes a brand to rank higher in ChatGPT;
visibility improvements increase sales; Google-specific advice
generalises to all platforms; vendor scores are universal truths.

## Candidate contribution (verify through further search)

A controlled India-focused comparison of Sephora, Nykaa and Tira across
beauty-shopping query intents, with repeated observations and
transparent citation coding, may be a useful applied contribution. Do
not claim "no prior study exists" unless a documented search supports
it.

## Review protocol

Search Google Scholar, ACM Digital Library, arXiv, publisher websites
and official platform docs. Log exact queries/date/filters, screen
titles/abstracts, read methods/results/limitations, separate
academic/official/vendor evidence, and record inclusion/exclusion
decisions. Cite APA 7 and update the search close to submission.
